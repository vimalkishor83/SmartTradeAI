No Guaranteed Returns or Predictions

Risk

Trading and investing in financial markets involves substantial risk, including the risk of losing your entire principal. No signal, confidence score, or AI-generated prediction shown on the Platform guarantees any particular outcome, return, or level of accuracy. Confidence scores reflect the output of a statistical model at a point in time — they are not a probability of profit and should never be treated as one. Markets are unpredictable, and even historically reliable strategies can fail without warning due to changing conditions.

Past Performance Is Not Indicative of Future Results

Risk

Any backtested results, historical win rates, equity curves, or performance statistics shown on the Platform reflect how a strategy would have performed on historical data under specific assumptions (fees, slippage, execution timing, data availability). Past performance — whether live or simulated — is not a reliable indicator of future results, and actual results may differ materially.
