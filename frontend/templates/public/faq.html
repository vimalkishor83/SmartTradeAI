Short answers about data, signals, plans, brokers and risk.

What does SmartTrade AI provide?

It provides market intelligence, explainable trading signals, multi-timeframe analysis, research tools and portfolio risk context across supported markets.

Are signals guaranteed?

No. Confidence measures internal indicator agreement. It is not a probability of profit, personalized advice or a promise of returns.

Do I need a broker?

No. The platform can be used as a read-only market-intelligence and paper-trading workspace. Broker connectivity is optional and user-controlled.

What does the Telegram feature do?

Personal signal notifications are configurable for eligible users. The group channel is reserved for curated market news and major market events.

How are performance results calculated?

Results use closed signal outcomes and should be read together with sample size, time period, market coverage and methodology.

Can I start without payment?

Yes. The Free plan provides a limited workspace so you can understand the product before choosing a paid capacity tier.
