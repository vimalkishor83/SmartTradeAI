THE WORKFLOW / FOUR STEPS

From noise to
next action.

The platform is designed like a calm research desk: every screen answers one question and moves you forward.

01

Discover

Start with the market pulse, heatmap, scanner and watchlist. See where price, sentiment and volatility deserve attention.

Output: a short list of assets worth investigating.
02

Validate

Compare timeframes, indicator agreement, provider freshness and market regime before treating a setup as meaningful.

Output: a signal with context, not just a direction.
03

Investigate

Open the AI Decision Inspector to understand the evidence, provenance, historical context and what could invalidate the setup.

Output: a decision you can explain to yourself.
04

Manage

Define entry, stop-loss, targets, expiry and exposure. Monitor the complete lifecycle without pretending confidence means certainty.

Output: a risk-aware plan or a clear decision to wait.
Important: confidence measures internal indicator agreement. It is not a probability of profit or personalized investment advice.
