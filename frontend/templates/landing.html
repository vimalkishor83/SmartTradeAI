Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Reach out from the dashboard once you're signed in.

How are the signals generated? ▸
Each signal runs through a multi-stage pipeline — trend, momentum, volume, and multi-timeframe alignment checks, plus a machine-learning confidence score — before it's shown. If a setup doesn't clear the bar, you'll see a HOLD instead of a forced call.
What markets and timeframes are covered? ▸
Crypto, Forex, Indian Stocks, Commodities, and Indices — across 7 timeframes from 1 minute to 1 day. Coverage varies slightly by plan (signal delay and watchlist size scale with your tier).
Do I need to connect a broker? ▸
No — you can use SmartTrade AI purely for signals and analysis. Connecting a supported broker is optional and only needed if you want one-click trade execution from inside the platform.
Is there a free plan? ▸
Yes. The Free plan includes delayed signals and a limited watchlist. Paid tiers unlock live (undelayed) signals, backtesting, AI insights, and larger watchlists — see the pricing table above.
Can I cancel or change my plan anytime? ▸
Yes — plan changes are requested from Settings once you're logged in and reviewed quickly. No lock-in contracts.
