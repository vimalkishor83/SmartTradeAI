Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Reach out from the dashboard once you're signed in.

What is SmartTrade AI? ▸
A market analysis platform that combines live market data, technical indicators, multi-timeframe analysis and AI-assisted scoring into a single signal — with an entry, stop-loss and target attached, so you can see the reasoning, not just a buy/sell flag.
How are the signals generated? ▸
Each signal runs through a multi-stage pipeline — trend, momentum, volume, and multi-timeframe alignment checks, plus a machine-learning confidence score — before it's shown. If a setup doesn't clear the bar, you'll see a HOLD instead of a forced call.
What does the confidence score mean? ▸
It reflects how many of the pipeline's checks agree with each other for that setup — higher agreement across trend, momentum and timeframe alignment scores higher. It's a measure of internal consistency, not a probability of the trade working out.
What markets and timeframes are covered? ▸
Crypto, Forex, Indian Stocks, Commodities, and Indices — across 7 timeframes from 1 minute to 1 day. Coverage varies slightly by plan (signal delay and watchlist size scale with your tier).
Do I need to connect a broker? ▸
No — you can use SmartTrade AI purely for signals and analysis. Connecting a supported broker is optional and only needed if you want one-click trade execution from inside the platform.
Is there a free plan? ▸
Yes. The Free plan includes delayed signals and a limited watchlist. Paid tiers unlock live (undelayed) signals, backtesting, AI insights, and larger watchlists.
Can I cancel or change my plan anytime? ▸
Yes — plan changes are requested from Settings once you're logged in and reviewed quickly. No lock-in contracts.
How is my account protected? Is 2FA supported? ▸
Passwords are never stored in plain text, and optional two-factor authentication (authenticator app or backup codes) can be enabled from Settings for an extra layer of protection on login.
I forgot my password — how do I get back in? ▸
Use Reset your password from the login page — we'll email a reset link if that address matches an account. The link expires after use for security.
Is SmartTrade AI financial advice? ▸
No. SmartTrade AI provides financial education, market information and analytical tools — not personalised investment advice. Always do your own research and consult a qualified financial professional before making financial decisions. See the full disclaimer.
Does SmartTrade AI guarantee profits or accuracy? ▸
No — no signal provider can. Every setup shown here can still be invalidated by the market. Trading involves substantial risk, including loss of principal; treat every signal as one input into your own decision, not a guaranteed outcome.
